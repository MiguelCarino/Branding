# Carino trademark policy

The code of every Carino Systems repository is free software under the licence in its
`LICENSE` file. The **name and the marks are not**. This policy applies to the whole fleet, to
every repository under github.com/MiguelCarino and every site under carino.systems.

## What the marks are

- the names **Carino** and **Carino Systems**, and the product names used with them
  (Carino Retina, Carino DICOM, Carino Offline, Carino Setup, Carino Images and so on);
- the gold **C** and every product mark built on it (`marks/` in this repository, and their
  copies in each site's `assets/marks/`, `logo.svg`, `logo.webp` and `og.jpg`).

They are trademarks of Miguel Carino (Carino Systems). The software licences cover the files
as copyright. They grant no right to use the name or the marks to identify another product
or service. The GNU AGPL says so in its section 7(e), and the MIT licence grants no trademark
rights at all.

## You may, without asking

- say truthfully that your work is **based on** or **compatible with** a Carino tool, in plain
  text, for example "a fork of Carino Topo";
- run an unmodified copy for yourself or your organisation, keeping the marks as shipped;
- write about, review or teach the tools using the names and marks.

## You may not, without written permission

- ship a **modified** version under the Carino name or with its marks. A fork must
  rebrand: give it a new name, a new icon, and remove the gold C;
- offer support, hosting, installation or certification as "Carino", or in a way that
  suggests it comes from or is endorsed by Carino Systems;
- use the name or a confusingly similar one in a product, company, domain or package name.

## Attribution is separate, and it stays

Rebranding removes the **marks**. It does not remove the **credit**: the licence and the
copyright notices ("Copyright © Miguel Carino") must stay with the code, as the licence
requires.
